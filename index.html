1. 项目目标、适用范围与总体架构

真实星空观测台 5.1.8 是一个完全本地化的浏览器天文馆。它不是随机星点动画，而是读取真实恒星目录、现代西方星座、中国传统星官、银河轮廓和太阳系天体数据，以观测者的经纬度、IANA 时区和 UTC 时刻为输入，计算并绘制当前天空。适合肉眼认星、天文文化比较、天球坐标教学和一般观测规划；不替代专业天体测量、精密历表、赤道仪自动指向或天文摄影规划软件。

中文显示说明：部分中国星官、星名和传统天区的上游资料采用繁体字。本版本已将面向用户显示的中文统一转换为简体字；仅转换文字，不修改恒星坐标、星官连线、目录编号或区域几何。

2. 地点、城市搜索、IANA 时区与时间

城市表 CITIES 保存中文名、英文名、WGS84 纬度、经度、IANA 时区和地区分组。搜索框同时匹配中文、英文和地区；选中城市后调用 setObserver()，同步经纬度、时区、状态栏和星图。手动输入经纬度时，resolveZone() 依次尝试显式时区、tzlookup(lat, lon) 和按经度估算的 Etc/GMT 回退。时区边界附近、海洋和少数复杂行政区域可能误判，因此界面将结果标为自动估计。

内部状态 state.instant 始终是 UTC ISO-8601 瞬时。日期输入框表示观测地点的当地民用时间，parseObserverLocalTime() 将年月日时分与当前 IANA 时区组合后转换为 UTC，并拒绝不存在的日期或夏令时跳变造成的无效当地时刻。切换地点只改变地点和显示时区，不改变 UTC 瞬时，因此表现为“同一时刻从另一个地点看天空”。shiftObserverTime() 在观测地时区内执行月、日、小时的日历运算，再转回 UTC；所以“+1 月”按目标月份实际天数处理，而不是固定加 30 天。

时间播放每帧只更新内部 UTC，HUD 与天球绘制分别节流；流速为 ×1、×60、×600、×3600 或 ×86400。当地时间、UTC、当前偏移量和 IANA 时区同时显示，夏令时由 Luxon 的 IANA 规则处理。

3. 天文坐标与本地天空计算

恒星目录以 J2000 赤道坐标为基础。赤经 α 沿天赤道向东计量，通常以时表示，1 h = 15°；赤纬 δ 类似地理纬度。观测时刻先转换为儒略日，再由地球自转得到格林尼治恒星时（GMST）；加上观测经度得到本地恒星时（LST），进而计算时角：

H = LST − α

由观测纬度 φ、赤纬 δ 和时角 H 得到高度角 h：

sin h = sin φ · sin δ + cos φ · cos δ · cos H

方位角由同一球面三角关系求得。本项目在天体信息框中调用 Celestial.horizontal(date, [RA, Dec], [lat, lon]) 获取当前高度角和方位角；显示约定为高度角 −90°～+90°，方位角 0° 为北、向东增加。地平视图由 Celestial.skyview() 按当前 UTC、经纬度和时区偏移旋转到当地天顶。赤经显示函数 formatRA() 把度转换为时、分、秒；赤纬使用带正负号的角度。代码不再根据数值大小猜测角度或弧度。

坐标视角与投影是两个独立概念。投影决定球面如何压到平面；坐标视角由“坐标渲染基准 transform”和“视角朝向 orientation”共同组成。地平坐标视角使用赤道 transform，再按当前地点和时间转到当地天空朝向；赤道坐标视角使用赤道 transform 和赤道默认朝向；黄道与银河坐标视角分别使用 ecliptic / galactic transform 和对应默认朝向。这里的 transform 是 D3-Celestial 的底层坐标变换，不是屏幕投影。

4. 投影、视角、缩放、拖动与响应式布局

项目提供 Airy 当地全天图、Orthographic、Stereographic、Azimuthal Equidistant、Azimuthal Equal Area、Aitoff、Hammer、Mollweide、Winkel Tripel、Equirectangular、HEALPix、Mercator、Robinson 和 Sinusoidal。方位类投影适合当地天空或局部视野；Aitoff/Hammer/Mollweide/Winkel Tripel 适合全天分布；圆柱和高级投影用于坐标教学或特定比较。地平线图层中的 E/S/W/N 是星图内部绘制内容，会随投影、拖动和缩放一起移动。

D3-Celestial 的 d3.geo.zoom() 负责 Canvas 内的滚轮定点缩放、鼠标左键拖动和触摸手势。项目另外把整个右侧星图区作为交互面：当投影因天然宽高比出现上下或左右留白时，forwardMarginWheel() 会把滚轮事件转发到实际投影画布的最近有效位置，beginPaneMarginDrag() / movePaneMarginDrag() 则允许在留白处继续拖动视野。attachCanvasInfo() 记录按下位置和移动距离：小于 6 px 视为点击选取，大于阈值视为拖动，不触发选取。拖动与缩放只改变投影中心和比例，不修改地点、时间或天体数据。captureView()、saveCurrentProjectionView() 和 restoreView() 为每个“坐标视角:投影”组合保存中心与缩放；语言、文化体系、图层开关和时间更新不重置视角。地平视图的 E/S/W/N 由自定义地平线图层沿真实地平方向绘制，不使用固定屏幕覆盖层。

每种投影使用自身的天然宽高比，而不是被浏览器窗口强制拉伸。projectionNaturalRatio() 读取 D3-Celestial 的投影元数据，未声明比例的全天投影按 2:1 处理；projectionCanvasMetrics() 根据星图区真实尺寸、天然投影比例和 mapScale 计算目标画布尺寸，因此圆形方位投影居中显示，Aitoff、Hammer、Mollweide 等全天投影完整展开。侧栏与星图区使用共享 Grid 布局；setPanel() 只切换列宽，ResizeObserver 触发 scheduleSkyResize()，用 Celestial.resize() 原位调整画布，不重载星表。地平与赤道坐标视角同属赤道 transform，切换时只恢复对应视角；黄道和银河坐标视角会切换 D3-Celestial transform，因此走安全重建路径，并由 preloaded-data.js 为每次数据请求提供深拷贝，避免重复转换污染目录几何。

投影与坐标视角必须分开理解：投影是“怎么画到屏幕上”；坐标视角是“用哪一套坐标渲染基准和朝向观察天球”。重置视图不会擅自切回赤道坐标：地平坐标会按当前地点和当前时刻重新计算本地天空中心，并只把缩放恢复为默认值；赤道、黄道和银河坐标则读取 src/config.ts 中各自的默认中心与缩放。菜单变化只触发布局尺寸同步，不改写投影公式或强行拉伸天球。

天球是球面，屏幕是平面，所以不存在“完全不变形”的全天图。投影只决定球面如何铺到屏幕上，不改变恒星的真实坐标。可以先按外观理解：Airy、正射、立体、方位等距和方位等积属于圆形或方位类视图，适合把某个方向放在中心观察；Aitoff、Hammer、Mollweide、Winkel Tripel 和等距圆柱属于全天展开图，适合比较整个天球的分布；HEALPix、Mercator、Robinson 和 Sinusoidal 更偏科学分区、局部保角或折衷展示。

5. 恒星、名称、星座、星官和区域图层

恒星图层使用约 6 等以内的目录。恒星显示条件是 m ≤ mlimit；它只是目录过滤器，不模拟光污染、天空背景、大气消光、月光或云。恒星半径由 D3-Celestial 按视星等、用户设定的大小和指数映射，B−V 色指数用于近似颜色。重要恒星名称是独立开关：西方模式下中文界面优先使用规范中文译名、英文界面使用英文专名；中国模式使用中国传统名称；双体系模式只保留中国名称，避免两套标签重复。

西方模式使用现代星座连线、名称和 IAU 88 星座边界。星座连线只是认星图案，不是法定边界；边界单独以低透明度虚线显示。中国模式使用独立的传统星官线和名称，不是把西方星座翻译成中文。双体系模式同时绘制蓝色西方连线与橙色中国星官线，但自动关闭区域边界，以避免画面过载。

中国传统天区采用分层表达：三垣（紫微垣、太微垣、天市垣）/ 四象（东方青龙、北方玄武、西方白虎、南方朱雀）/ 二十八宿细分 / 近南极星区。三垣边界依据相关垣墙星官的数字化几何生成；四象和二十八宿宿域依据各宿中心经度的中点构造；近南极星区表示明清时期吸收并整理的南天星官区域。这些都是为网页比较而制作的现代数字化复原，不是像 IAU 边界那样唯一、法定的历史边界。

“南方战场、北方战场、西北战场”作为传统星官叙事中的军事主题区域单独显示：北方战场参考垒壁阵、羽林军、北落师门、天纲、天垒城、八魁等星官；西北战场参考昴、毕、天街、天大将军、军南门、五车等；南方战场参考翼、轸、角、亢、氐、房、心以及青丘、库楼、骑官、阵车等。代码用这些星官的已知位置生成低透明度凸包，只表示“主题星官分布范围”，不宣称存在统一古代边界。每个 GeoJSON 要素都带有 approximate、source 和 note 字段。

中国古代还存在其他上层组织方案，例如《史记·天官书》的中宫、东宫、南宫、西宫、北宫“五官”框架，以及后世整理中出现的“四兽加中官、外官”“五兽加中官、外官”“九野加中外官”等分类。这些体系的名称、时代、覆盖范围和边界定义并不统一，当前也缺少可直接用于现代天球坐标的标准化公开多边形，因此本版把它们收入说明而不伪造几何边界。未来若引入明确版本的古星图或学术复原数据，应作为可选择的独立历史图层，而不是与三垣四象混为一套。

6. 图层绘制、标签管理与天体选取

buildSkyConfig() 根据状态生成恒星、深空天体、西方星座、银河、网格、黄道、天赤道等原生配置，并启用项目自定义地平线和地平坐标网图层； D3-Celestial 的本地太阳系历表数据但关闭其不完整的原生符号绘制。registerPlanetOverlay() 通过本地兼容补丁暴露的历表对象，调用 currentPlanetPositions() 以当前 UTC 和地球位置计算太阳、月球及七大行星的赤道坐标，再按当前 D3-Celestial transform 转换、投影并绘制符号和名称；同一组动态坐标也用于点击拾取和信息框。普通开关通过 Celestial.apply() 原位更新；投影通过 Celestial.reproject() 更新；坐标视角如果切换了 D3-Celestial transform，会从深拷贝目录数据安全重建。中国星官和传统天区使用 Celestial.add() 注册自定义 GeoJSON 图层，在每次 redraw 中依据文化模式、边界开关和细分层级决定是否绘制。

中国星官名称和传统天区标签使用屏幕距离避让：绘制前把天球坐标投影为像素点，若与已占用标签距离过近则跳过低优先级标签。恒星专名由引擎按星等限制；未来仍可加入统一的跨图层碰撞管理器，目前不能保证极高密度视野中所有标签都完全不重叠。

点击选取由 selectAtEvent() 完成。nearestCatalogObject() 在动态太阳系对象、恒星、深空天体、西方星座名和中国星官名中计算屏幕距离并使用不同命中半径；未命中时通过当前公开投影对象的 invert() 把鼠标像素反投影为天球坐标。由于 D3-Celestial 0.7.35 在 reproject() 后会替换内部投影对象却不更新公开的 mapProjection 引用，本项目对本地固定版本加入一行兼容补丁，使自定义星官、行星和点击拾取始终使用当前投影。信息框显示当前体系和语言下的名称、类型、视星等、目录编号、B−V、RA/Dec、当前 Alt/Az、地点和时间；月球额外显示照明比例、月龄和距离，行星可显示历表提供的星等和距离。点击复制可导出当前信息；切换地点或时间后，updateSelectedObject() 重新计算动态坐标。

1. Purpose, scope and architecture

Real Sky Observatory 5.1.8 is a fully local browser planetarium. It is not a random star field: it reads real stellar, constellation, Chinese asterism, Milky Way and Solar System datasets, combines them with the observer's coordinates, IANA time zone and UTC instant, and renders the resulting sky. It is intended for naked-eye orientation, sky-culture comparison, coordinate teaching and general observing preparation, not precision astrometry, telescope control or professional ephemeris work.

2. Observer, city search, time zones and time

CITIES stores Chinese/English names, WGS84 coordinates, IANA zones and regional groups. Search matches either language and selecting a city calls setObserver(). Manual coordinates are resolved by explicit zone, tzlookup(lat, lon), then a longitude-based Etc/GMT fallback. Compressed lookup boundaries can be wrong near borders or at sea, so the UI identifies the result as an estimate.

state.instant is always a UTC ISO-8601 instant. The input field represents local civil time at the observer. parseObserverLocalTime() combines the entered fields with the current IANA zone, converts to UTC and rejects impossible dates or DST-gap normalization. Changing location preserves the UTC instant. Month/day/hour controls operate in the observer's calendar zone and then return to UTC, so a month is not approximated as 30 fixed days.

3. Astronomy and coordinate conversion

The stellar catalog is based on J2000 equatorial coordinates. Right ascension α is commonly expressed in hours, with 1 h = 15°; declination δ is analogous to latitude. The local hour angle is:

H = LST − α

For observer latitude φ, the altitude h follows:

sin h = sin φ · sin δ + cos φ · cos δ · cos H

The information panel uses Celestial.horizontal(date, [RA, Dec], [lat, lon]) to calculate current altitude and azimuth. Azimuth is displayed from north through east. Celestial.skyview() rotates the local horizon view for the current UTC instant and observer. The code uses the documented degree convention and never guesses radians from numeric range.

Coordinate view and map projection are separate. A coordinate view combines a D3-Celestial transform with a viewing orientation: horizontal uses the equatorial transform with the local-sky orientation, equatorial uses the equatorial transform with its default orientation, and ecliptic/galactic use their matching transforms and default orientations. The transform is the coordinate rendering base, not the screen projection.

4. Projections, view state and interaction

Available projections are Airy, Orthographic, Stereographic, Azimuthal Equidistant, Azimuthal Equal Area, Aitoff, Hammer, Mollweide, Winkel Tripel, Equirectangular, HEALPix, Mercator, Robinson and Sinusoidal. E/S/W/N are shown only for local horizon-compatible views. The whole sky pane is the interaction surface: D3's geographic zoom handles cursor-centred wheel zoom, left-button panning and touch gestures inside the Canvas, while letterboxed margins forward wheel events to the nearest valid Canvas point and use the same saved view state for pointer dragging. A 6-pixel movement threshold separates clicking from dragging.

Each projection keeps its natural aspect ratio instead of being stretched to the browser. projectionNaturalRatio() reads engine metadata and projectionCanvasMetrics() fits the largest complete Canvas into the pane; undeclared global projections use the conventional 2:1 ratio. E/S/W/N are attached to the actual Canvas boundary. Each coordinate-view/projection pair has its own saved centre and zoom. Projection changes use Celestial.reproject(); ordinary display changes use Celestial.apply(); layout resizing uses Celestial.resize() without catalog reload. Horizontal/equatorial coordinate view switches only restore centres and scales. Ecliptic/galactic view switches change the D3-Celestial transform and therefore rebuild from deep-copied local catalog data.

4A. A beginner’s guide to the 14 projections

A celestial sphere is curved while the screen is flat, so every all-sky projection has distortion. Projection changes only how coordinates are drawn, not the underlying star positions. Airy, Orthographic, Stereographic and the two azimuthal projections are centre-oriented views; Aitoff, Hammer, Mollweide, Winkel Tripel and Equirectangular are all-sky layouts; HEALPix, Mercator, Robinson and Sinusoidal serve scientific partitioning, local conformality or balanced presentation. Reset never changes the selected coordinate view: horizontal mode recalculates the local sky centre from the current observer and instant, while equatorial, ecliptic and galactic modes use their own configured defaults. E/S/W/N are drawn by the custom horizon layer, so they move with the projected sky instead of staying fixed on the browser edge.

5. Stars, names and sky cultures

The stellar limit is a catalog filter, m ≤ mlimit, not a light-pollution model. Symbol size is mapped from magnitude, while B−V supplies an approximate colour. Name logic follows the selected sky culture: Western mode uses standard Western names (Chinese translations in the Chinese UI, proper English names in English); Chinese mode uses traditional Chinese names; combined mode keeps Chinese star names only.

Western constellation figures, labels and IAU boundaries are independent layers. Chinese asterisms use separate geometry and names, not translated Western constellations. Combined mode overlays blue Western and orange Chinese figures but disables regional boundaries.

Overlapping Western/Chinese segments: in combined mode, ordinary segments stay in their original positions. Segments with matching endpoints are rendered as two slightly separated screen-space tracks, with the Western line shifted to one side and the Chinese line to the other. The offset is visual only and never changes catalog coordinates or source geometry. Very short shared segments use phase-shifted short dashes; edge-clipped segments fall back to centered rendering when a stable offset cannot be calculated.

Cultural notes for important stars: when a bright important star is selected, the information card reads short explanations of its Western constellation and Chinese asterism from the project-owned cultural notes bundled from src/data/culture-notes.ts. These are learning-oriented summaries rather than primary-source quotations; mythic and historical interpretations can vary.

The Chinese region layer contains the Three Enclosures, Four Symbols, optional Twenty-Eight Mansion subdivisions and a near-south-polar zone. Their polygons are documented modern digital reconstructions, not IAU-like legal boundaries. The Northern, Northwestern and Southern Battlefields are thematic envelopes derived from the positions of related military asterisms. They are intentionally labelled approximate and must not be interpreted as a unique historical boundary system. Other historical organizing schemes—including the Five Offices of the Shiji, later Four-Beasts-plus-central/outer, Five-Beasts-plus-central/outer, and Nine-Fields classifications—are documented but not drawn because their versions and boundaries are not standardized into a reliable modern celestial polygon dataset.

6. Rendering, labels and object information

buildSkyConfig() assembles native star, DSO, Western constellation, Milky Way, grid, ecliptic and equator layers, while custom reference layers draw the horizon and horizontal grid. The bundled Solar System ephemeris is exposed to registerPlanetOverlay(). currentPlanetPositions() computes the Sun, Moon and seven planets for the current UTC instant and Earth origin, then projects, draws and exposes the same equatorial-base positions for picking. registerChineseOverlay() and registerTraditionalRegionsOverlay() add custom GeoJSON. Chinese labels use simple screen-distance suppression; this is not yet a global collision solver.

nearestCatalogObject() tests dynamic Solar System objects, stars, DSOs, Western labels and Chinese labels with type-specific pixel radii. Empty-sky clicks are inverted through the current projection. A local compatibility patch updates D3-Celestial 0.7.35's public mapProjection reference after reproject(), keeping custom overlays and picking aligned with the visible map. The sidebar card displays culture-aware names, type, magnitude, identifiers, B−V where present, RA/Dec, current Alt/Az, observer and time; the Moon additionally exposes illumination, age and distance. Dynamic coordinates are recalculated after time or location changes.

9. Offline behavior, performance and scientific limitations

The root index.html loads generated assets/app.css and assets/app.js, plus local astronomy data from vendor/. Source files and external resources are retained in src/ and vendor/. Geolocation may be blocked under file://; python -m http.server 8000 provides a localhost context. Manual coordinates and all sky functions remain offline.

Limitations: this is not a full high-precision astrometric propagation pipeline. It does not model arbitrary-epoch stellar proper motion, parallax, full precession/nutation, aberration and relativistic terms to research-grade precision. It does not model atmospheric refraction/extinction, sky brightness, light pollution, moonlight, clouds, weather, altitude, terrain or a measured horizon. Solar System positions are intended for general planetarium display. The Milky Way is an outline. Chinese region and battlefield polygons are documented reconstructions, not unique historical legal boundaries.

Possible extensions include object search, rise/transit/set, lunar phase, twilight, visibility curves, higher-precision ephemerides, proper-motion propagation, weather/light-pollution/terrain layers, mobile sensors and multiple historical Chinese sky reconstructions.
